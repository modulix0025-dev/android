Dialect sentence pairs from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org)

The dialect corpora in this app (assets/tm/*.tsv) contain sentences and translations from the
Tatoeba project, released under the Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France licence (CC BY 2.0 FR):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/deed.en

Attribution: every pair taken from Tatoeba names, in its "provenance" field, the Tatoeba sentence
ids and the usernames of the contributors who wrote the dialect sentence and its translations
(https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>). The sentences were normalised only in whitespace; pairs
were selected (length, script, duplicate checks) but not modified.
