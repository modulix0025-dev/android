Dialect sentence pairs from Google SMOL (https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/smol)

SMOL (SmolSent, SmolDoc) and GATITOS are professionally translated data published by Google under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribution: every pair taken from SMOL names its subset, the dataset revision and the item id in its
"provenance" field. The pairs were selected (length, script, duplicate checks) but not modified.
