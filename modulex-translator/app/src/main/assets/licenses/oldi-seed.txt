Dialect sentence pairs from OLDI Seed (https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlanguagedata/oldi_seed)

OLDI Seed is managed by the Open Language Data Initiative (https://oldi.org) and is an updated version of
the NLLB Seed dataset ("Small Data, Big Impact: Leveraging Minimal Data for Effective Machine
Translation", ACL 2023). Its expert translations of English Wikipedia sentences are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Attribution: every pair taken from OLDI Seed names the dataset revision and the sentence id in its
"provenance" field. Share-alike: the corpus files that contain these rows (assets/tm/*.tsv) are licensed
under CC BY-SA 4.0. This applies to those data files only, not to the application code.
